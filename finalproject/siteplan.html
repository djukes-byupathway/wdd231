Site Plan

1. Site Name

Vault of Jukes

This name was selected to make it personal to the two founders, brothers who wanted to make something. Also it evokes thoughts of treasure, protected and safe, but waiting to be plundered by daring adventurers.

2. Site Purpose

This site is intended to be the home page for a budding collaboration of two brothers aiming to develop and provide content to fellow gamers, specifically around traditional Role Playing Games (RPGs) and table top miniature games.

3. Scenarios

  • what is Vault of Jukes?
  • how can i stay in the loop on what Vault of Jukes is working on?
  • what products/services do you offer?
  • who are you guys again?
