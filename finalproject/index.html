What Are We About?

Original Settings

We create fantasy worlds filled with mystery and adventure, from the perpetual night of Dark World to the Jungles of Veilagh, to the magical destopian setting of Old Hexburg.

Innovative Mechanics

Doesn't everyone love house rules? We are digging into our vault of resources and plan to share the best of our home grown content as well as game mechanics that are pluggable into existing campaigns and settings.

Collaborative Community

After spending years of gaming with our friends from high school and college, we are branching out and broadening our reach. We invite all of you to join in our adventures and will look for ways to share with you via multiple channels and mediums.
